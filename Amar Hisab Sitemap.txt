## 2. Sales (বিক্রয়)
- **POS Sale** – Main point-of-sale screen
- **Quick Sale** – Streamlined sale with minimal steps
- **Wholesale Sale** – Bulk sale with wholesale pricing
- **Retail Sale** – Standard retail transaction
- **Sales History** – List and search past sales
  - Sale Detail (items, payments, batches used)
  - Invoice Print / Reprint
  - Receive Payment (due collection)
  - Cancel / Void Invoice
- **Draft Invoices** – Unfinished sales
- **Hold Sale** – Suspended transactions
- **Quotations / Estimates** – Create and manage quotes
  - Convert Quotation to Sale
- **Delivery Notes** – Manage deliveries against sales
  - Update Delivery Status
- **Sales Return** – Process customer returns
- **Exchange** – Product exchange workflow
- **Installment Sales** – Sales with installment plans
  - Installment Schedule
- **Credit Sale Management** – Manage credit sales and limits
- **Receive Payment** – Standalone due collection

---

## 3. Purchase (ক্রয়)
- **New Purchase** – Record goods received
- **Purchase History** – List and search past purchases
  - Purchase Detail (items, payments, batches received)
- **Purchase Orders** – Create and manage POs
  - Convert PO to Purchase Invoice
- **Pending Purchase** – Orders not yet received
- **Import Purchase** – Bulk import via Excel
- **Purchase Return** – Return goods to supplier
- **Supplier Payment** – Record payments to suppliers
- **Supplier Invoice** – Direct entry of supplier bills

---

## 4. Products (পণ্য)
- **Product List** – Master list with search, filter, and bulk actions
  - Add / Edit Product
- **Categories** – Manage product categories
- **Sub Categories** – Manage sub-categories
- **Brands** – Manage brands
- **Generic Name** – (Pharmacy) generic medicine names
- **Manufacturer** – (Pharmacy) manufacturers list
- **Batch Number** – Manage batch tracking settings
- **Barcode** – Generate and assign barcodes
- **QR Code** – Generate product QR codes
- **Warranty** – Manage product warranties
- **Unit** – Units of measurement
- **Color** – Product colors (clothing)
- **Size** – Product sizes (clothing)
- **Variant** – Product variants (size, color, etc.)
- **Serial Number** – Track individual serial numbers
- **Product Images** – Manage product gallery
- **Product Notes** – Internal notes

---

## 5. Inventory (মজুদ / স্টক)
- **Current Stock** – Real-time stock overview
  - Filter by warehouse, category, batch
- **Low Stock** – Items below reorder level
- **Out of Stock** – Zero quantity items
- **Damaged Product** – View and manage damaged goods
- **Lost Product** – Record lost inventory
- **Expiring Product** – Batches nearing expiry
- **Expired Product** – Expired batches
- **Batch Wise Stock** – Stock grouped by batch number
- **Warehouse Stock** – Stock per warehouse/branch
- **Stock Adjustment** – Manual stock corrections
- **Stock Transfer** – Transfer stock between warehouses
- **Barcode Label Print** – Print product/batch barcode stickers
- **Inventory History** – Audit trail of all stock movements

---

## 6. Customers (ক্রেতা)
- **Customer List** – Search, filter, add customers
  - Customer Detail (profile, transactions, due)
- **Customer Groups** – Segment customers (retail, wholesale, VIP)
- **Customer Due** – Report of outstanding customer balances
- **Customer Ledger** – Individual account statement
- **Loyalty Points** – Manage loyalty programme and redemption
- **Membership** – Membership tiers and benefits

---

## 7. Suppliers (সরবরাহকারী)
- **Supplier List** – Search, filter, add suppliers
  - Supplier Detail (profile, transactions, due)
- **Supplier Due** – Report of outstanding supplier balances
- **Supplier Ledger** – Individual account statement
- **Supplier Statement** – Periodic statement of account

  - Service Charge
  - Miscellaneous Income

## 9. Pharmacy (ঔষধ ব্যবসা) *(shown if business type = Pharmacy)*

- **Generic** – Generic drug names
- **Strength** – Drug strengths
- **Dosage** – Dosage forms
- **Manufacturer** – Pharmaceutical manufacturers
- **Batch** – Batch tracking for medicines
- **Expiry** – Expiry date management

- **Schedule Medicine** – Controlled/H‑schedule drugs

- **Student Attendance** – Daily attendance marking

  - Attendance Reports
- **Exams & Results** – Record exam marks and grades
- **Student Secrets** – Manage portal passwords
- **Student Portal QR** – Generate public QR for self‑service
- **Digital Student ID** – Generate personal QR codes for students
- **Fee Collection** – Fee payment recording (integrated with Sales)
- **Student 360° Profile** – Complete student view (fees, attendance, results, enrollment history)
- **Certificate Print** – Generate and print course certificates
